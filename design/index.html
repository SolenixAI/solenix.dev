The rule

One ring, one dot.

Two dots is a different mark. The dot always breaks the ring on the upper right — it is one agent in orbit, and it sits outside the sun so it stays visible at small sizes.

Numbers are always mono and tabular, so columns line up.

Prose is capped at 34rem. Long lines are the most common way to make plain words feel hard — a reader who has to track back across a wide column reads slower and trusts less, even when every word is simple.

Headings balance; paragraphs wrap pretty. No headline is left with one word on its last line.

Components

Every state defined as a foreground and background pair. Contrast after a hover is never lower than at rest.

A link, inline in prose

One solid button per action, per screen. Every other entry point is secondary, ghost, or a text link — and never the same words twice. Ghost and secondary keep --text on hover and move the background instead; the primary swaps fill and ink together.
