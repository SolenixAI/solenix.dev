Questions

What people ask first

What does it cost?

It depends on what we build. After our call you get a written plan with a fixed price for the build and a monthly price for looking after it. You'll know the full cost before anything starts.

How long does it take?

Small jobs, like an assistant for common questions or a website refresh, usually take a few weeks. Bigger custom software takes longer. Your plan gives you a date, and the portal shows progress as we go.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain words. You tell us how your business works; we do the rest.

Is my customers' information safe?

Yes. We only use the access a job needs, we keep your data in your accounts where we can, and we'll walk you through exactly what goes where.

What if something breaks?

If we look after it, we watch it. When something goes wrong we usually know before you do, we fix it, and we tell you what happened.

Do you only work with businesses nearby?

No. We work remotely with small businesses anywhere in Canada and beyond.
