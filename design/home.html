Questions

What owners ask me first

What does it cost?

It depends what you need, so I won't guess on a web page. After one call I'll send you a fixed price for the whole job, in writing. If you don't like the number, you've lost twenty minutes.

How long does it take?

A small assistant or a straightforward site is usually a matter of weeks, not months — but I'll give you an actual date in the plan rather than a range now. You'll see progress as it happens in your portal.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

No. You're the one who knows how the business runs — that's the part I can't work out on my own. I'll explain everything in plain words, and I won't ask you to make a decision you don't have the information for.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

Usually not. Often the quickest win is leaving the site alone and putting an assistant behind it. I'll tell you honestly if the existing site is holding you back.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name from the start — domain, hosting, accounts, code. If we stop working together, nothing needs to be handed over, because it was never mine.

What happens if it breaks?

You call or email me and I fix it. If it's down, I'll tell you it's down, what I'm doing and when I'll know more. No status page dressing it up.

Tell me what's eating your week.

One call, no charge, no pitch. If I'm not the right person for it, I'll say so and point you at who is.
