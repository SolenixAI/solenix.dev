What we do

Five moves, in that order

Most of the value is in the first two. Nobody sells you software you do not need.

1 · Sort out what you have

We go through every tool, login and subscription you are paying for and write down what it does, what it costs, and whether anything depends on it. Most owners have never seen that list. It is usually the most useful hour of the whole engagement.

You get the list whether or not you hire us for anything else.

No jargon, no audit theatre — a plain list you can read.

Move 2

Cut the sprawl

Cancel what nothing depends on, merge what overlaps, and keep what genuinely earns its place. Fewer logins, fewer bills.

Move 3

Build what is missing

Only the gap that is left after cutting — a site, a booking flow, an automation. Fixed price, agreed first.

Move 4

Run it

We host it, watch it, patch it and fix it. You do not get handed a server and a login.

Move 5

One place to see all of it

Your portal: what is running, what we are building, what it costs. No chasing anyone for a status.

Questions

What owners ask first

I already pay for a lot of tools. Will this cost more?

Often it costs less. The first thing we do is list what you are paying for and what each thing actually does; cancelling the overlap usually covers a meaningful part of our fee. We will tell you honestly if it does not in your case.

Do I have to throw out what I already have?

No. Anything that works and earns its place stays. We are looking for the tools nothing depends on, the ones you pay for twice, and the ones nobody can log into — not a reason to rebuild everything.

What does it cost?

It depends what you need, so we will not guess on a web page. After one call you get a fixed price for the whole job, in writing. If you do not like the number, you have lost twenty minutes.

How long does it take?

A straightforward site or a small app is usually a matter of weeks, not months — but the plan gives you an actual date rather than a range. You watch progress in your portal as it happens.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

No. You are the one who knows how the business runs, which is the part we cannot work out on our own. Everything gets explained in plain words, and we will not ask you to decide something you do not have the information for.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

Usually not. Often the quickest win is leaving the site where it is and putting something behind it. If the existing site is genuinely holding you back, we say so.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name from the start — domain, hosting, accounts, code. If we stop working together, nothing needs to be handed over, because it was never mine.

What happens if it breaks?

We monitor it, so usually we know first and it is fixed before you ask. When something is down you get told it is down, what we are doing and when we will know more — not a status page dressing it up.

Send us the list of what you are paying for.

One call, no charge, no pitch. You get the plain list of what you have either way, and if we are not the right fit we say so and point you at who is.
