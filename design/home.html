The journey, in words

  1. Revenue, costs and time, pulled apart by a dozen tools. We put one AI at the centre of the tools you already use. Hours back, every week.
  2. Each tool you run eats hours you do not get paid for. That is where this starts.
  3. We set up the AI agent of your choice for your team and connect it to the tools you already use. You say what you want in plain words, and it gets done in those tools.
  4. Example: you ask who owes you money. Hillside Roofing is 64 days late; three clients owe $4,450. Then you ask to invoice Harbour Dental for the retainer, and the invoice is ready for you to check before it goes.
  5. Example: you ask to sort today's inbox. Four emails need a reply today, nine can wait, sixteen are junk. Then you ask to book 30 minutes with Harbour Dental: Tuesday at 10:30, and the invite waits for your OK.
  6. Example: you ask what runs out this month. Sea salt caramels and wool mitts will run out within 30 days. Then you ask to reorder them, and both reorders are drafted for you to approve.
  7. Example: you ask which ad is wasting money. Local awareness now costs far more for each sale than it did. Then you ask to move its budget to the fall sale, and the change waits for your call.
  8. Example: you ask what sold best this month. Bread and coffee are up; pastry slipped. Then you ask for a chart, and it is added to your sales sheet.
  9. On the call, we add up what your tools cost you, in hours and dollars.
  10. You stop paying for the ones you barely use. The rest move off outdated software.
  11. We set up the AI agent of your choice and connect it to your tools. Then we teach your team to use it.
  12. It is where your customers decide to call you. We look after it. You own everything.
  13. Agreed on the call. No surprise invoice.
  14. Fixed price, in writing, agreed on the call. You own it all: accounts, code and data in your name. Based in St. John's. Your team is taught to use it.
