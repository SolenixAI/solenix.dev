16 · 19 · Rules

Scene and conversion, at a glance.

Do

  • Fit the camera so the whole loop shows at every aspect ratio.
  • Put scene text on the veil, never on bare canvas.
  • Show the Motion switch on any page with motion over 5 seconds.
  • Name who it's for in under eight words.
  • Repeat “Book a call” at logical points, one in view at a time.
  • Draw the figure-eight from the real path in section 17.

Don't

  • Pin a scene with position: fixed, or let the sun core pass under text.
  • Show chaos inside the portal, or end a page unsettled.
  • Print a number you haven't committed to. Use the chip.
  • Use logos, testimonials, invented metrics or scarcity.
  • Let scene labels touch, or clip them at 375px.
  • Draw an ∞ glyph or a lemniscate in place of the figure-eight.
